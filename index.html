How it works

Three steps, and you only do them once.

  1. Install it on your computer

    On a Mac or Linux computer, open Terminal. On Windows, open PowerShell. Paste one line and run it. It takes about two minutes, needs no administrator password, and ends by showing a code.

    The line to paste will be here soon.

  2. Scan the code with the app

    Open Sendstone on your phone, choose Connect my computer and point the camera at the code. No camera? Type the six numbers shown under it instead.

  3. Use your AIs from anywhere

    Start a chat on your phone and pick it up at your desk: the same chats show up on every device, live. At home your phone talks to your computer directly. Away from home it still works, encrypted end to end.

What it works with

The AI tools you already use on your computer, and two ways to chat from the phone alone.

  • AI tools on your computer

    Claude Code, Codex, Gemini, Ollama and others, on the plans you already pay for, right next to your files. Sendstone shows you the ones it finds. Claude Code and Codex can even be signed in from your phone: you sign in on the company's own page, and Sendstone never sees your password.

  • AI services, with your own key

    Chat from your phone straight to Anthropic, OpenAI, Google and others with your own API key. The key stays in your device's keychain and goes only to that service.

  • A model on the phone itself

    Download a free model that runs on the phone with no internet at all. Nothing leaves the device.

The computer can be a Mac, a Windows 10 or 11 PC, or a Linux computer. The app is for iPhone, iPad, Mac and Windows.

Your chats stay yours

Privacy, in plain words.

  • No account

    There is nothing to sign up for. Your phone pairs with your computer once, by scanning a code shown on its screen.

  • Only your devices

    Only the devices you pair can use your computer. You can remove any of them at any time, and it is cut off at once.

  • Encrypted end to end

    Your phone and your computer talk over a sealed connection. At home it goes straight between them. Away from home it goes through a relay that passes it along, cannot read it and stores nothing.

  • Nothing collected

    No analytics, no ads, no tracking. Your chats stay on your own devices. What you ask an AI goes to the company that makes it, as it would anyway.
